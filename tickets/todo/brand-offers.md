# Brand offers (N products, € off)

Captured: 2026-09-10

Related: `tickets/done/loyalty-stamps-on-ticket.md`

## Goal

Run promotions such as: two products from a brand, X euros off.

## Context

This is the promo engine. Loyalty redemption (−5 €) is not this; it is a ticket line, not a brand rule.

Do not bolt brand offers onto victorines.

## Done when

A cashier can sell a matching basket and see the discount apply without a one-off script. Rules are stored so every till on the LAN sees the same offer.
