# Till operator guide

This guide is organised by everyday situations at the counter. Open the use case that matches the customer's request and follow the steps in that document. The examples use feminine language for the customer and the saleswoman.

## Use cases

1. [A customer wants to buy normally](use-cases/normal-sale.md)
2. [A product is not found after scanning](use-cases/product-not-found.md)
3. [A customer wants several units or a correction](use-cases/ticket-correction.md)
4. [A customer wants to be identified on the ticket](use-cases/customer-on-ticket.md)
5. [A customer wants to use loyalty](use-cases/loyalty.md)
6. [A customer wants to pay with a gift voucher](use-cases/pay-with-voucher.md)
7. [A customer wants to buy a gift voucher](use-cases/buy-gift-voucher.md)
8. [A customer wants a gift receipt](use-cases/gift-receipt.md)
9. [A customer wants to pay in parts](use-cases/split-payment.md)
10. [A customer wants the drawer opened without a sale](use-cases/open-drawer.md)
11. [A sale must be continued later](use-cases/park-ticket.md)
12. [A completed sale must be corrected or returned](use-cases/refund-or-edit.md)

Stock management, two-till operation, cash closing, reports, and the shop LAN will be documented separately.
