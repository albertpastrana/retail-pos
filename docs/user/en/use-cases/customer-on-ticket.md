# A customer wants to be identified on the receipt

**Situation:** The customer wants the sale attached to her customer record, usually to consult debt or receive loyalty-related assistance.

## Find the customer

1. Press the customer action in the sales actions row above the receipt.
2. Search by name or phone.
3. Compare the result with the customer before selecting it.
4. Confirm the selection. The customer chip appears on the receipt.

## Create the customer

If there is no matching result, choose **New customer**. Enter both the name and phone; both fields are required. Confirm the form to attach the new customer to the current receipt.

Phone numbers are searched after spaces are removed. A phone number is not guaranteed to be unique, so check the name before selecting a result.

## Correct or remove the customer

- Use **Change** when the wrong customer was selected.
- Use **Remove** to clear the receipt without deleting the customer.
- Open the customer sheet from the chip to see the customer's debt and available debt-payment action.

Customer debt is separate from the paper loyalty balance.
