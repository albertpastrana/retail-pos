# A customer wants to buy a gift voucher

**Situation:** The customer wants to leave with a voucher that somebody else can use later.

## Sell the voucher

1. Start a new receipt if the customer is not buying anything else.
2. Find and add the gift voucher product.
3. Check the value and quantity. Gift vouchers are sold in whole units.
4. Press **Equals** and complete the payment normally.
5. Give the issued voucher or its receipt to the customer.

Do not treat the voucher sale as a loyalty redemption. A voucher sale does not earn loyalty stamps.
