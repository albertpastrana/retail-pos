# A customer wants to buy normally

**Situation:** A customer has one or more products and wants to pay in the usual way.

## 1. Open the sale

1. Enter the sales screen.
2. The screen opens with a current receipt. If the saleswoman needs a new receipt, use the **New receipt** action before scanning anything.
3. Check the seller shown on the receipt. The seller buttons are in the top row above the receipt, beside the other sales actions. Each button shows the seller's image or initials and her name or shortcut below it.

## 2. Choose the saleswoman

The saleswoman can click her seller button. She can also use the shortcut printed under that button:

- The first visible seller is `F1`.
- The second visible seller is `F2`, and so on up to `F9`.
- `F10` is not a seller shortcut. Sellers after the ninth one must be selected with the mouse.

The shortcut belongs to the position of the visible seller, not to the seller's name. If the saleswoman changes seller, the current receipt is saved and the receipt for the selected seller is opened or created. Selecting a seller is therefore also how she moves to that seller's receipt; it is not just a label change.

## 3. Scan the products

1. Leave the cursor in the code field and scan the first barcode.
2. Check that the product line appears in the receipt.
3. Check the product name, price, size, colour, and quantity.
4. Scan the remaining products one by one.

The scanner adds one unit for each known product barcode. If the customer has several units, correct the quantity after scanning or scan the barcode again.

## 4. Finish the receipt

1. Review every line and the total.
2. Press **Equals** on the numeric keypad, or press the equivalent total key.
3. The payment window opens. Choose the payment method there; payment methods are not selected directly on the receipt screen.
4. Complete the payment and press **OK** in the payment window.
5. After the receipt is saved, the till prints the receipt when printing is enabled.

**Remember:** Correct a quantity or remove a line before pressing **Equals**. After payment, use the refund or edit use case instead.
