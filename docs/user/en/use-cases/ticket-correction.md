# A customer wants several units or a correction

**Situation:** The receipt has a wrong quantity, an unwanted line, or a discount.

## Change the quantity

1. Select the product line.
2. Use the quantity controls on the keypad, or scan the product again to add another unit.
3. Check the new quantity and total.

## Remove a line

1. Select the line that should not be sold.
2. Press the delete action.
3. Check that the line and its total have disappeared.

Line editing requires the corresponding sales permission. If the action is unavailable, ask a supervisor instead of working around it with a new receipt.

## Apply a discount

- Use a line discount when only one product is discounted.
- Use a total discount when the discount applies to the whole receipt.
- A sale percentage already assigned to a catalogue product appears on its line. It is a line discount, not a payment and not loyalty-eligible.

Review the total and the loyalty message after changing a discount.
