# A customer wants a gift receipt

**Situation:** The customer wants a receipt to accompany a present without showing the normal price details.

## Print the gift receipt

1. Complete the sale and payment normally.
2. In the receipt actions row above the receipt, press **Gift ticket**.
3. Check that the gift receipt prints.
4. Give it to the customer together with any ordinary receipt required by the shop's procedure.

The gift-receipt action prints the current completed receipt in the gift format. It does not create a gift voucher and it does not change the payment.
