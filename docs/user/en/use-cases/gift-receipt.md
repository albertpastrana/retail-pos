# A customer wants a gift receipt

**Situation:** The customer wants a receipt to accompany a present without showing the normal price details.

## Print the gift receipt

1. Complete the sale and payment normally.
2. In the receipt actions row above the receipt, press **Gift ticket**.
3. Select the lines that should appear on the gift receipt and confirm. Do not leave the selection empty.
4. Check that the gift receipt prints.
5. Give it to the customer together with any ordinary receipt required by the shop's procedure.

The gift-receipt action prints a selection of lines from the completed receipt in the gift format. You can print different selections for different recipients. The loyalty redemption line is not included on the gift receipt. It does not create a gift voucher and it does not change the payment.
