# A customer wants to pay with a gift voucher

**Situation:** The customer already has a gift voucher and wants to use its remaining balance.

## Use the voucher

1. Press **Equals** on the sales keypad after checking the receipt.
2. In the payment window, select the voucher payment tab.
3. Enter or scan the voucher code.
4. Check the available balance shown by the till.
5. If the voucher covers the whole amount, press **Use voucher** and then **OK**.

## When the voucher is partial

If the voucher does not cover the whole receipt, the till shows the amount that remains:

1. Press the partial-payment action to add the voucher amount.
2. Select cash, card, or another payment tab.
3. Enter and add the remaining amount.
4. Press **OK** only when the pending amount is zero.

If the voucher is not found, empty, or cannot be checked, do not guess its value. Stop the payment and ask the person responsible for the till.

**Remember:** Using a voucher as payment is different from adding the `-5 EUR` loyalty line.
