# Management reports

**Situation:** Staff need to review sales or stock without blocking the till.

## Available reports

The reports menu currently includes:

- **Sales summary:** receipts, gross sales, refunds, net sales, tax, and average receipt.
- **Sales by product:** reference, product, category, units, and amount.
- **Sales by payment method:** sales, refunds, and net amount by payment method.
- **Low stock:** current, minimum, and maximum stock, plus units to order.

## Choose a period

1. Open the relevant report.
2. Select the start and end dates.
3. Press **Load**.
4. Review the results and sort tables when needed.

Reports may take a moment because they query the database. Do not close the application while a report is loading.

Reports grouped by model, cash-closing summaries, and CSV export are part of the reporting rebuild and should not yet be treated as available features.
