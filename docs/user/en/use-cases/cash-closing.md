# Close and review cash

**Situation:** The shift is ending and staff need to close the cash register.

## Close cash

1. Open **Close till** from the authorised menu.
2. Review the sales, payments, and cash movements shown on screen.
3. Count the physical cash and enter the count when requested.
4. Review the difference and summary before confirming.
5. Confirm the close and keep the printed receipt or summary when printing is enabled.

Loyalty redemption is a discount, not a separate payment method. Refunds and mixed payments must be included in their corresponding summary.

## Backup during closing

A backup can run automatically during cash closing when configured. It can also be created from **Maintenance** on the Linux till. Confirm that the file reaches the backup location and copy it according to the shop procedure.

## Review closed cash

Use the closed-cash view to review an earlier closing. Do not reopen or correct a closed cash by repeating the sale; use the ticket edit or refund flow for a ticket mistake.
