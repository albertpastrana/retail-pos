# A customer wants to pay in parts

**Situation:** The customer wants to use more than one payment method, such as a voucher plus card or cash plus card.

## Add the first payment

1. Press **Equals** after checking the final receipt.
2. Select the first payment tab.
3. Enter the amount to apply.
4. Press the partial-payment or add-payment action. Do not press **OK** yet.
5. Check the remaining amount shown by the payment window.

## Add the remaining payment

1. Select the second payment tab.
2. Enter the remaining amount.
3. Add the payment.
4. Continue until the pending amount is zero.
5. Press **OK** to confirm the complete mixed payment.

The receipt prints after the complete payment is confirmed. The same flow applies when a gift voucher covers only part of the receipt: add the voucher first, then pay the remainder with another method.
