The product grid's unit, on the till's sales screen and, unchanged, on a future website's catalogue or feature grid. Shows exactly three things: the product name (`body-lg`), its price (`price`, right- or bottom-aligned so figures line up down the grid), and — on the till only — a `StatusBadge` when stock is low or the item is out of stock. Nothing else: no description, no secondary metadata, in a grid a cashier is scanning at speed.

Sits on `surface-100` with `shadow-raised`, `radius-md` corners, `space-4` internal padding. The tile itself is the tap target on the till (the whole card adds the line, not a button inside it) — give it the same `space-12` minimum on its shorter axis as any other primary touch target. On hover/press, lift only by swapping to `shadow-overlay`; never add a border or recolour the tile — a coloured left border on a card is exactly the decorative use of colour this system avoids (see `README.md` → Colour).
